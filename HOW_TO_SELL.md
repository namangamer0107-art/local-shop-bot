# 🚀 Local Shop Pitch & Onboarding Checklist (Selling Guide)

Use this step-by-step checklist to close your first 3 local businesses this week.

---

## 🎯 Target Businesses with Highest Close Rates
1. **Unisex Salons & Barbershops** (High call volume during haircuts)
2. **Custom Cake Bakeries** (Endless inquiries on flavors, kg pricing, delivery)
3. **Dental & Physiotherapy Clinics** (Need appointment booking without dedicated receptionist)
4. **Auto Detailing / Garage** (Price quote and slot booking inquiries)

---

## 🤝 Step-by-Step Selling Workflow

### Step 1: 5-Minute Setup Before You Walk In
1. Go to Google Maps and pick a target shop (e.g., *"Star Unisex Salon"*).
2. Open `tenants/` and duplicate `salon_demo.json` to `star_salon.json`.
3. Fill in their real:
   - Business name
   - Real opening hours & address from Google Maps
   - 3 or 4 services with real prices from their menu
   - Owner's phone number

### Step 2: Show the Live Demo on Your Phone
Walk into the shop or WhatsApp the owner directly:
> *"Hey [Owner Name], I love your salon! I noticed when you're busy with a client, you often miss calls or customer WhatsApp messages. I built a quick 30-second WhatsApp assistant for [Shop Name] that automatically shows your menu, answers timings/parking, and lets customers book slots directly into your calendar.*
> 
> *Test it yourself right now on my phone (or scan this QR). Just send 'Hi'."*

### Step 3: Show the Shop Owner Dashboard
When they test booking an appointment on WhatsApp:
1. Open your laptop or mobile browser at `http://<your-ip>:8000/dashboard?tenant_id=star_salon`.
2. Show them their booking appearing instantly in their dashboard with customer name, phone, service, and slot.

## 📋 What You Need From The Shop to Go Live
Once they say **YES**:
1. Their Business Phone number (can be their existing SIM or a new cheap \$2 SIM card).
2. Facebook Business Manager access (or register the number under Meta Cloud API - takes 10 mins).
3. Their full list of services and prices.
